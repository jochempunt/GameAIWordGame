Scientists Explain[1]

Participate in our research study

You are invited to take part in a Utrecht University research study.

You will play an online word-based game in which players create answers to prompts and rank answers based on how funny they find them. We are studying whether AI models can predict these human rankings.

We will record your game activity, including the prompts you receive, answers you submit, and rankings you provide.

Please take as much time as you need to read the Informed Consent Document before deciding whether to participate. If you have questions, or later wish to withdraw and request deletion of your data, contact the researchers at example@example.com.
